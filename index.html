FAQ

Good to know.

Where do sessions take place?

Most sessions happen in Miami, Miami Beach, Doral and Hialeah. I can also travel to other locations within South Florida for an additional fee — just let me know where you have in mind.

How long until I receive my photos?

Portrait and couples sessions are delivered within 7 days. Maternity and event sessions within 10 days. You'll receive a private online gallery with high-resolution downloads and print-ready files.

How do I book a session?

Fill out the contact form, send me an email or message me on WhatsApp. Once we agree on the details, a 50% deposit reserves your date. The remaining balance is due on the day of the session.

What should I wear?

Once your date is booked, I'll send you a personalized guide with outfit ideas, color palettes and location suggestions based on the mood you want for the session.
